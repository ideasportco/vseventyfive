# Case Memory and Rule Governance

1. Raw outcomes do not automatically modify the skill's trading rules, thresholds, or verdicts.
2. A journal entry is a user-reported observation until independently reconciled with candle and execution data.
3. Lessons are proposals. A reviewer records the evidence, sample definition, limitations, and whether the lesson is accepted for the reference library.
4. Do not promote a lesson from a single winning or losing trade. Avoid selection bias by preserving losing, missed, ambiguous, and no-trade cases.
5. Any rule change requires a versioned proposal, a predeclared evaluation protocol, out-of-sample testing, review, and a documented decision.
6. Keep training/reference cases separate from blind evaluation cases. Do not use evaluation outcomes to tune the same version and then report them as independent evidence.
7. Historical performance cannot prove future profitability. Any paper or live trading is a separate decision and should use risk limits appropriate to the user.
8. Remove personal identifiers and account credentials from images and journal records.
