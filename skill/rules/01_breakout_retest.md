# Rulebook 01: Breakout and Retest Mechanics

## 1. Theoretical Foundation
This rule synthesizes Laurentiu Damir (*Price Action Breakdown*) and the *Simple Trading Book*. Classical breakout trading suffers from high false-breakout rates (whipsaws), especially in high-volatility synthetic indices like V75. 

To achieve high win-rate and asymmetric expectancy, we never enter on the initial breakout impulse. We enter exclusively on the **retest confirmation**.

## 2. Anatomical Stages

### Stage A: Pattern Boundary Definition
- A valid structural level requires at least **two distinct rejections** (touches) to qualify as a confirmed support, resistance, or neckline.
- Acceptable patterns:
  - Necklines of Double/Triple Tops and Bottoms
  - Necklines of Head & Shoulders formations
  - Triangle upper/lower bounds
  - Rectangle channel extremes

### Stage B: The Breakout Candle
- A valid breakout requires the **candle real body to close definitively outside** the boundary.
- **Disqualifications**:
  - Wick-only penetration: If the candle pierces the line with a wick but closes back inside, it is an invalid breakout (potential trap/false break).
  - Exhaustion candle: If the breakout candle is abnormally massive (> 3x average ATR), the move is likely exhausted. Wait for deeper retest or pass.

### Stage C: The Retest Leg
- Retest must occur within 2 to 12 candles following the breakout.
- Price must pull back to touch or closely approach the broken boundary.
- **In-Zone Behavior**: Candle bodies must respect the broken boundary. If a candle body closes significantly back inside the original structure, the breakout is marked **FAILED** and trade setup is discarded.

### Multi-Timeframe Alignment
- Where available, H1 swing levels dictate the macro boundary, while M5 provides the granular retest and entry trigger.
- Trading with the macro trend (e.g., buying broken resistance in an H1 uptrend) is prioritized over counter-trend retests.
